# Help

## Signing in with Bluesky

Make sure to keep your App Password safe for ATtune, as ATtune uses it to access data associated with your Decentralised Identifier (DID).

## Key Features of ATtune

- Record albums you have listened to, rate them out of 5 starts and write a review!
- View your and other users profiles and reviews.
- Share reviews on your Bluesky profile for your users to see.
- Comment and like other users reviews.
- Discover and explore artists, tracks and albums through recommendations on the Home and Explore pages.
- Edit your tastes of your top artists and tags to help with recommendations.
- Search for users, artists and albums.

## How to use ATtune

### Create your User Tastes:

- Visit your Profile page.
- Click on the Edit Tastes button.
- Click on the input fields and start typing the name of an artist or tag.
- Click on the correct artist name/tag.
- Repeat until you have added all your top artists and tags.
- Click on the 'Save Tastes' button to save your tastes.
- Your tastes should be saved on your profile! Your recommendations should change accordingly.

### Record an Album:

- Visit the Record page.
- Click on 'Type an album or artist name...' to start typing the name of an album or artist.
- Click on the correct album.
- Rate the album out of 5 stars.
- Write a review of the album. (Optional)
- Edit the date listened to the album. (Optional)
- Check or uncheck the 'Publish to Bluesky timeline' checkbox to publish your review to your Bluesky timeline. (Already checked by default)
- Click on the 'Post Review' button to save your review.
- Your review should be saved on your profile! If you checked the box to publish to Bluesky, your review will also be posted on your Bluesky profile to others for them to like and comment.

## FAQs

**Q: How do I delete all my data?**

A: You can delete all your data on record through the settings page. Disclaimer: ATtune does not guarantee the deletion of your data as it is stored on a decentralised network.

**Q: How do I see other user profiles?**

A: You can see other user profiles by searching for their username in the search bar of the navbar. If they have utilised ATtune, you will be able to see their tastes and review history.

**Q: Why does ATtune need my App Password?**

A: ATtune works within the ATmosphere and is built atop the AT protocol. Therefore, for it to utilise decentralised features such as allowing users to own their own data, an app password is required.

**Q: Where does all the music data come from?**

A: All music data is retrieved from the Last.fm API. ATtune does not handle the accuracy of the data.
